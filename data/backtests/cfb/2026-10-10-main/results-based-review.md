## Initial conclusions

1. The optimizer found useful ceiling plays, particularly at quarterback and
   running back.
2. Wide-receiver and thin-role value selection was the largest weakness in this
   slate. Two lineups used Derrick Salley Jr., who scored zero.
3. Portfolio leverage was too aggressive in the second and third DraftKings
   lineups. A three-entry portfolio should include at least one more balanced
   construction rather than making every lineup highly contrarian.
4. Unique lineups are desirable, but uniqueness alone did not create value here;
   all six entries were unique while none reached the top quartile except the
   best DraftKings lineup narrowly missing it.
5. Future validation must preserve the generated run archive or projections CSV
   alongside the submitted lineups. That is required to determine whether the
   misses came from projections, ownership estimates, or portfolio selection.
