# Week 4 entered lineups

These files reconstruct the lineups entered for the 2026 Week 4 slates from
the recorded roster lists. They preserve player names and roster slots for
backtesting; they are not contest-upload templates.

Assumptions:

- The first player listed for each single-game lineup is the FanDuel MVP or
  DraftKings Captain.
- `Kittle Higbee` in DraftKings Classic lineup 1 was interpreted as two
  players: George Kittle at TE and Tyler Higbee at FLEX.
- Slot context and archived salary-cap validation resolve shortened names such
  as `Allen` to Josh Allen, `Love` to Jeremiyah Love, `Wilson` to Michael
  Wilson, and `Robinson` to Wan'Dale Robinson.
- The saved DraftKings single-game salary file is for IND-WAS rather than the
  DET-CAR game represented here, so DraftKings player IDs cannot be recovered
  from the current archive. Names are sufficient for later score matching.
