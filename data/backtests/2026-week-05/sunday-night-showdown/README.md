# NFL 2026 Week 5 — Sunday night Showdown

Keep every artifact from the Sunday-night single-game slate in this folder.

- `salary-files/`: the original DraftKings and FanDuel salary CSVs.
- `generated-lineups/`: every optimizer export or complete run ZIP, including
  lineups that were not entered.
- `entered-lineups/`: the final rosters actually submitted.
- `results/`: final contest standings CSV or ZIP exports.

Keep DraftKings and FanDuel files even if only one platform was entered. Do not
overwrite earlier generated runs; the differences help us audit projection and
news changes.
